I built a RetroPie arcade machine. I didn't record video, but I did take a few pictures along the way.

For most of the wood to wood attaching I used Titebond glue and an 18 gauge brad nailer.

[![RetroPie Arcade Joystick Box Inner Framing](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-inner-framing.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-inner-framing.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Deciding where to put the joystick and button holes was not easy and I did not end up with something I was totally happy with. Maybe I was over-thinking it.

[![RetroPie Arcade Joysticks & Buttons Holes](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joysticks-buttons-holes.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joysticks-buttons-holes.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Some of the angles were fun to figure out. Having a compound miter saw helped a lot.

[![RetroPie Arcade Joystick Box Sideview](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-sideview.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-sideview.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Inside the back you can see the 19" Dell monitor I stripped down and mounted with wood blocking. This particular monitor had a 5:4 ratio, which works great for older console games. I used a hot glue gun to attach the monitor controls to the back of the monitor.

There's also the not yet mounted sound board dangling.

[![RetroPie Arcade Electronics Rough-In](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-electronics-rough-in.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-electronics-rough-in.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Everything is wired up and mostly working. No paint yet. I wasn't sure if I would get this far.

[![RetroPie Arcade Working Pre-Paint](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-working-pre-paint.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-working-pre-paint.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Here is an inside view of the joystick box. Each button and joystick attach to a control module that connects to the Raspberry Pi via USB. The second joystick has to be mounted upside down. I don't recall why.

[![RetroPie Arcade Joystick Box Wiring](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-wiring.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-wiring.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

I moved the joysticks and some of the buttons. I refilled the old holes with scrap wood and epoxy.

[![RetroPie Arcade Moved Button Fills](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-moved-button-fills.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-moved-button-fills.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

I hate painting. I hate taping things up before painting even more. It's just boring.

[![RetroPie Arcade Joystick Box Black Paint](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-black-paint.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box-black-paint.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

The black paint is done and it's time to tape things up in preparation for the red paint.

[![RetroPie Arcade Black Paint Work](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-black-paint-work.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-black-paint-work.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

More red paint prep work.

[![RetroPie Arcade Prep For Red Paint](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-prep-for-red-paint.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-prep-for-red-paint.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Red paint in progress.

[![RetroPie Arcade Red Paint Work](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-red-paint-work.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-red-paint-work.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Red paint is done on the main cabinet piece.

[![RetroPie Arcade Painted](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-painted.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-painted.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

The joystick box is ready for a clear coat, to lock in the button labels.

[![RetroPie Arcade Joystick Box](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-joystick-box.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

The 4" speakers are pretty loud for their size. I usually have the volume knob on "3" when I'm playing.

[![RetroPie Arcade Speakers Installed](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-speakers-installed.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-speakers-installed.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Marquee is installed and it's pretty much complete.

[![RetroPie Arcade Playing Wizards & Warriors](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-playing-wizards-warriors.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-playing-wizards-warriors.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

[![RetroPie Arcade Closed Back View](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-closed-back-view.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-closed-back-view.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

"Guitar" volume knob. It goes to "11".

[![RetroPie Arcade Side View](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-side-view.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-side-view.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Inside the back, everything is cleaned up and properly mounted.

[![RetroPie Arcade Back Inside View](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-back-inside-view.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-back-inside-view.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Power bus and switch wiring. 20 gauge wire. Lots of hot glue gun.

[![RetroPie Arcade Power Panel](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-power-panel.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-power-panel.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

The Raspberry Pi is mounted with Velcro®.

[![RetroPie Arcade Inside Bottom View](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-inside-bottom-view.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-inside-bottom-view.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

The volume knob outside connects to a 12" extension inside that connects to the potentiometer on the sound board.

[![RetroPie Arcade Inside Top View](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-inside-top-view.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-inside-top-view.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

The power strip is also mounted with Velcro®.

[![RetroPie Arcade Power Supply](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-power-supply.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-power-supply.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

USB ports for keyboard and mouse. Makes setup much easier being able to open a browser and Google problems. Switches for the fan and marquee lighting.

[![RetroPie Arcade USB Plug Outlets & Power Switches](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-usb-plug-outlets-power-switches.jpg){width=640}](/assets/img/retropie-arcade-usb-plug-outlets-power-switches.jpg){target=_blank rel=noopener}

Main power. Inside I soldered all the switch terminals for safety.
